# STOPP/START Criteria Version 3 Summary

## Principles of STOPP Criteria
STOPP (Screening Tool of Older Persons' Prescriptions) Version 3 provides physiological-system-based criteria identifying medications where clinical risks outweigh therapeutic benefits in older individuals. Prescribing decisions must account for life expectancy, functional capacity, renal clearance, and polypharmacy burden.

## Cardiovascular System Interactions
1. Anticoagulants combined with NSAIDs: Concomitant prescribing of direct oral anticoagulants or warfarin with systemic NSAIDs produces unacceptable gastrointestinal hemorrhage risks and is strongly discouraged.
2. Dual antiplatelet therapy: Aspirin combined with clopidogrel or other antiplatelets beyond indicated post-ACS or stenting windows increases fatal bleeding without cardiovascular benefit.
3. Beta-blockers combined with verapamil or diltiazem: Significant hazard of complete atrioventricular heart block and profound bradycardia.
Source: STOPP/START Version 3 (2023) - Cardiovascular Criteria

## Renal and Musculoskeletal Systems
1. NSAIDs with baseline eGFR < 50 mL/min: Severe acceleration of renal decline and hyperkalemia.
2. Long-term NSAID therapy in osteoarthritis: Paracetamol, topical treatments, or physiotherapy should precede chronic oral NSAID regimens.
3. Systemic corticosteroids with NSAIDs: Multiplying peptic ulcer and upper GI bleeding incidence.
Source: STOPP/START Version 3 (2023) - Renal and Musculoskeletal Criteria

## Central Nervous System and Fall Precautions
1. Tricyclic antidepressants in patients with dementia, glaucoma, cardiac conduction defects, or prostatism: Contraindicated due to potent anticholinergic toxicity.
2. Vasodilators and alpha-blockers in patients experiencing persistent orthostatic hypotension.
3. Antipsychotics for non-psychotic behavioural and psychological symptoms of dementia: Substantial stroke and mortality risk.
Source: STOPP/START Version 3 (2023) - Central Nervous System Criteria
